## WARNING (READ, DO NOT SKIP!)
- This is only for the **Steam** version of **Grand Theft Auto IV: The Complete Edition**. You can't remove the Rockstar Game Launcher from the Rockstar Game Launcher version of GTA IV.

- This disables achievements. Achievements are triggered through Rockstar Games Launcher, without that, you can't unlock achievements. If you want achievements, you just have to keep Rockstar Games Launcher.

- This mod moves the save directory from the `Documents` folder to a dedicated `save` folder in your `GTAIV` folder. If you have saves, you'll have to move them to `Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Grand Theft Auto IV\GTAIV\save\GTA IV`.

- If you need to report bugs to mod authors, clarfiy that you're using this mod when describing the issue.

## Introduction
Rockstar Games Launcher causes a lot of problems for people, it's easily the biggest complaint people have when using The Complete Edition of GTA IV. Sometimes it won't let you sign in, it often crashes the game on launch and it's also a nightmare to deal with on Steam Deck.

Thankfully we have a way to remove it with a mod called **RGLess**.

## Download & Installation

1. Download RGLess from your preferred source.

    - [Mediafire](https://www.mediafire.com/file/q1zejgje6qr7app/RGLessIV.zip/file){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [Mega](https://mega.nz/file/PjxjzKKR#9KbMnB0qqmxsMEdTdDZ1M0B1ldw5M2Ie6I3A8VPTePE){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} or [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/rgless-iv){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

2. Open your game folder and rename `GTAIV.exe`, `PlayGTAIV.exe` and `binkw32.dll` to `GTAIVbak.exe`, `PlayGTAIVbak.exe` and `binkw32bak.dll`.

    !!! tip
        If you're not sure how to find your game folder...

        - **Steam**: You can find it by right clicking on `Grand Theft Auto IV: The Complete Edition` in your Steam library, going to `Manage` and then clicking `Browse Local Files`. The `GTAIV` folder is where `GTAIV.exe` is located.

        - **Steam Deck/Machine**: Launch the Steam application while on your desktop, locate `Grand Theft Auto IV: The Complete Edition` in your Steam Library, right click (`Left Trigger` on controller) on the game to open the context menu, go to `Manage` and then click (`Right Trigger` on controller) `Browse Local Files`. The `GTAIV` folder is where `GTAIV.exe` is located.
    
3. Open the archive you just downloaded and then copy and paste everything from the archive into your game folder where `GTAIV.exe` is located.

## Uninstallation

1. Delete `GTAIV.exe`, `PlayGTAIV.exe` and `binkw32.dll`.

2. Rename `GTAIVbak.exe`, `PlayGTAIVbak.exe` and `binkw32bak.dll` back to `GTAIV.exe`, `PlayGTAIV.exe` and `binkw32.dll`.
