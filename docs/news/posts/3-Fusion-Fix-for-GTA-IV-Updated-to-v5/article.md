## Introduction

Fusion Fix 5 is now available for Grand Theft Auto IV. This update brings new features, fixes and overall improvements.

## Changes

Here's a quick look at some of the new features...

- Console gamma now affects UI
- Added manual indicator controls as an .ini option
- Ambient occlusion is now available as a graphics option
- Added a new option for skipping the fade effect when changing/buying clothes
- Many frame rate related fixes, especially in several of the games mission scripts
- Drivers on the Algonquin Bridge will no longer swerve out of the way of the train below the road
